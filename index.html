The Deccan, 1336–1875

500 years in the Deccan: from the founding of Vijayanagara and the Bahmani sultanate to the Company takeover and the Deccan Riots of 1875. The story of a plateau where sovereignty was repeatedly shared and inherited, until one power made itself paramount and reduced the rest to a register.

About

A Fragmented Peninsula is a study site maintained by M.C. Naniwadekar: collections on the history and natural history of the Deccan. The choice of which maps and entries to cover is the collector’s. The text was first drafted with AI and is being rewritten by hand as time allows; it remains a work in progress. It has been cross-checked for accuracy, every entry names its sources, and each collection’s About page says how it was put together and checked. However, no site of this size can be guaranteed free of error, and corrections are welcome at the address below.
