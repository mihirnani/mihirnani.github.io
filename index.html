Curiosities

Collections

The European Gaze on India

Four centuries of European maps of India, from 1519 to 1946, arranged as a thematic and chronological exploration of cartography, commerce, survey, empire, and knowledge.

About the collection

These projects began as ways of exploring things that interested me. Some are collections and works of curation; others are courses, games, and interactive tools. They will continue to change as I learn, verify, and revise.

Get this as an app

Any of these projects can live on your iPhone or iPad Home Screen and open full-screen, like an app. Each one also works offline after it has been opened once.

  1. Open the project you want in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ↑ — the square with an upward arrow.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Its icon appears with your other apps.

On Android, open the project in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
