Collections

The European Gaze on India

Sixty-five European maps of India, 1519 to 1946, with a 1947 epilogue, arranged in seven rooms as an argument about cartography, commerce, survey and empire.

Visit →

The Deccan, 1500–1830s

A timeline of the Deccan from Vijayanagara to the Company in seventy-six entries – battles, treaties, people, places, documents – arranged in five periods with the polities as a filter, and linked to the maps.

Visit →

Birds of the Sahyadris

A searchable field guide to the birds of the Western Ghats, with species notes, classification, status, and photographs. Still growing, and still being checked.

Visit →

Games

StarWeaver

An exploration-strategy game set in a fictional survey cluster within the real Milky Way. Chart the cluster, build a Captain’s Atlas, recover three Star Keys, and find the Celestial Gate. Every voyage generates a new chart.

Math Quest

A maths adventure for children aged 7–10: short lessons, generated practice, quizzes and rewards along a structured path from place value to fractions, geometry and measurement.

Toybox

Eight quiet mini-games for memory, pattern, geometry, number sense, colour and recursive forms, each with a short note on the idea behind it. Progress stays on your device.

Hellenike – Ancient Greek

A self-study course from the alphabet to a first reading of Homer: Attic grammar built stage by stage, exercises and spaced repetition, morphology tables and principal parts, a short history of Greece, and a graded reading room.

Use these as apps

Each project can sit on a phone or tablet Home Screen and open full-screen, like an app, and each works offline once it has been opened.

  1. Open the project in Safari (iPhone or iPad) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Tap Share ↑ in Safari, or the ⋮ menu in Chrome.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen (or Install app).

Built by hand, hosted on GitHub Pages. The map and bird collections are open, non-commercial study projects; image credits are given on each page.
