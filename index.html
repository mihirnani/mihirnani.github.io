The European Gaze on India

European maps of India, 1519 to 1946, with a 1947 epilogue, arranged in seven rooms as an argument about cartography, commerce, survey and empire.

The Deccan, 1336–1875

Five centuries of the Deccan, from the founding of Vijayanagara and the Bahmani sultanate to the Deccan Riots of 1875, in a collection of entries – battles, treaties, people, places, documents – arranged in seven periods with the polities as a filter, and linked to the maps.

The making of the Deccan: three and a half billion years of the plateau – craton, Gondwana, the Trap eruptions, laterite and the black soil – in a collection of entries ending in the landscape on which the Deccan’s human history was made.
