About

Curiosities is a study site kept by M.C. Naniwadekar: collections on the history and natural history of the Deccan and the Western Ghats. The choice of which maps and entries to cover is the collector’s. The text was first drafted with AI and is being rewritten by hand as time allows; it has been cross-checked for accuracy, every entry names its sources, and each collection’s About page says how it was put together and checked. No site of this size can be guaranteed free of error, and corrections are welcome at the address below.
