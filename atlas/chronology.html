One chronology

Deep time from Basalt and Laterite, then human time from 1296 to 1947: the Deccan chronology, the Deccan entries, and the maps of The European Gaze, in one order of years.

Deep time · 3,600 million years to the present

Each panel of the band is one of the seven periods of Basalt and Laterite; within a panel the positions run on a logarithmic scale from older, on the left, to younger. Ages are in years before the present, as the collection stores them; a ruler could not hold them.

Human time · 1296 to 1947

The Deccan chronology's own items, the entries that have no item of their own, the last entries of Basalt and Laterite, and every European map, by year. Items marked Entry and Map link to their pages; the rest are context, as in the chronology itself.
