Places

Every place that anchors an entry in The Deccan, 1336–1875 or Basalt and Laterite, with its coordinates, its entries, and – where the map collection has indexed it – the European maps that show it.

The index

Names are the collections' own, spelling variants merged and localities folded into their town; the state is kept. Coordinates are those the entries carry, averaged where a town has several.
